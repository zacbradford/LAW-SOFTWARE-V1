How it works

Three steps, not a black box

01

Paste or attach

Drop in the contract as text, or attach a .txt file. No formatting or cleanup needed.

02

Matched to a review skill

You pick the document type; Flagstone applies that type's specific checklist — leases, NDAs, loan agreements, and eight others.

03

Flagged, not buried

Get a short summary plus every clause worth a second look, each rated by severity, then ask follow-up questions in the same thread.

This is an early, internal test build — said plainly, not buried in fine print

  • The review skills are drafted from general legal knowledge and haven't been checked yet by a practising Australian lawyer.
  • Every output carries a disclaimer: AI-generated, may contain errors, a qualified lawyer must review it before anyone relies on it.
  • Access is limited to a small group of invited testers while this gets validated.
